Your information matters

We believe privacy should be simple and transparent. This policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, how it may be used and the choices available to you.

01 — INTRODUCTION

A little clarity before we begin

This Privacy Policy explains how {{ site_settings.site_name|default:"VOYAGE" }} handles information collected through our website, accounts, enquiries, bookings and related services.

By using our website or submitting information to us, you acknowledge that your information may be handled as described in this policy.

02 — INFORMATION

Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with VOYAGE, we may collect information such as:

  • Your name and contact details.
  • Account information such as your email address and login-related information.
  • Traveller details provided during a booking.
  • Destination, tour and booking preferences.
  • Messages, enquiries and feedback you send to us.
  • Payment-related information required to process or verify a transaction.
  • Technical information about how you use our website, where applicable.
03 — PURPOSE

How We Use Your Information

We may use information we collect to provide, maintain and improve our services.

  • Create and manage your account.
  • Process and manage bookings.
  • Send booking confirmations and important updates.
  • Respond to questions, enquiries and support requests.
  • Improve our website, services and user experience.
  • Detect, prevent or investigate misuse and security issues.
  • Meet applicable legal or administrative obligations.
04 — BOOKINGS

Your Booking Information

When you make a booking, we need certain information to identify the traveller, manage the reservation and provide the requested travel service.

This may include names, contact information, travel dates, selected tours, traveller counts and other details necessary to complete your booking.

Please make sure that information submitted during the booking process is accurate and belongs to you or that you are authorised to provide it.

05 — PAYMENTS

Payment Information

Payments may be processed through third-party payment providers.

Depending on the payment method used, payment information may be collected and processed directly by the relevant payment provider rather than being stored by VOYAGE.

Payment providers may have their own privacy policies and terms governing how payment information is handled.

06 — COOKIES

Cookies & Similar Technologies

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies to support essential functionality, remember preferences and understand how visitors interact with the website.

Some cookies may be provided by third-party services used by the website.

Your browser may provide controls for managing or restricting cookies. Disabling certain cookies may affect parts of the website.

07 — SHARING

When Information May Be Shared

We do not treat your personal information as something to casually share. Information may be shared when reasonably necessary to provide our services.

  • With travel suppliers involved in fulfilling your booking.
  • With payment providers when processing transactions.
  • With service providers supporting our website, communications or operations.
  • When required by applicable law or a valid legal request.
  • Where necessary to protect the security, rights or property of VOYAGE or others.
08 — SECURITY

Keeping Your Information Safe

We take reasonable measures to protect information against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure or destruction.

However, no online service or method of electronic transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. You should therefore avoid sending sensitive information through unsecured communication channels.

09 — RETENTION

How Long We Keep Information

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide our services, maintain records, resolve disputes, meet legal obligations and protect our legitimate interests.

The exact retention period may depend on the type of information and the reason it was collected.

10 — YOUR CHOICES

Your Rights & Choices

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights concerning the personal information we hold about you.

  • Request access to certain personal information.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion where legally applicable.
  • Ask questions about how your information is used.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent, subject to applicable limitations.

To make a privacy-related request, please contact us using the details provided at the end of this policy.

11 — CHILDREN

Children's Privacy

Our services are intended to be used with appropriate involvement of a parent, guardian or responsible adult where required by applicable law.

We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children in circumstances where such collection is not permitted.

12 — THIRD PARTIES

Links to Other Websites

Our website may contain links to third-party websites, including travel suppliers, social platforms and payment services.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices, security or content of third-party websites. We recommend reviewing their respective privacy policies before providing personal information.

13 — COMMUNICATIONS

Emails & Service Communications

We may send transactional communications relating to your account, enquiries, bookings, payments or other services you have requested.

These communications are important to the operation of your booking and may still be sent even if you choose not to receive optional promotional messages.

14 — UPDATES

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our services, technology, legal requirements or privacy practices.

When changes are made, the updated version will be published on this page together with a revised "Last updated" date.
